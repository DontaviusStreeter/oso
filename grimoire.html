Gravesbury Park · Personal Grimoire

Oṣó

The Blood Witch · Dontavius “Dopeboy” Streeter
Blood · Inheritance · Debt · Consequence
“Blood is not merely what keeps you alive. It is what connects you to everything that came before you... and everything you leave behind.”

This grimoire records Donta’s personal supernatural capabilities. It is separate from Hex & Herbals’ private commissions, prepared goods, dolls, gris-gris, potions, and ritual services. Incantations are original roleplay flavor rather than established Gravesbury canon.

I. His Signature Abilities

Blood manipulation · Advanced

1. Vein Command

Donta exerts control over another person's movements by influencing the blood within their body. He can force a hand to open, make a knee buckle, interrupt a step, or briefly hold a target in place. He doesn't need to puppeteer an entire body to make the ability dangerous. A small, precisely timed interruption can be enough to change a confrontation.

“Blood, hear my command.”

Limits: Requires intense concentration. Fine, sustained control is harder than a brief movement. Strong resistance, distance, and multiple targets increase the burden on him.
Pain induction · Advanced

2. The Unwounded Agony

Donta can induce severe pain through blood magic without cutting, bruising, or otherwise visibly injuring his target. The effect can be a sudden wave of agony or a sustained, punishing ache that makes concentration and resistance difficult. The body appears untouched even while the victim struggles to function.

“No blade shall mark you. No wound shall show. Let the blood remember what the flesh won’t know.”

Limits: Causing intense pain is not the same as inflicting physical damage or automatically rendering someone unconscious. Maintaining the effect drains him, especially against a determined target.
Hemokinesis · Intermediate

3. The Red Stillness

Donta can slow or stop bleeding, or accelerate it through blood magic. This makes him useful in emergencies and exceptionally dangerous in a fight. He can also recognize when bleeding has been magically altered rather than occurring naturally.

“Flow when I bid. Be still when I speak.”

Limits: This isn't Life Witch healing. Stopping blood loss doesn't repair damaged tissue, restore lost blood, cure an illness, or guarantee survival. Complex injuries require greater effort and concentration.
Physical enhancement · Intermediate

4. Bloodforged Physique

Donta channels blood magic through his own body to temporarily increase strength, endurance, stability, and resistance to fatigue. He can also weaken a target's physical capacity through a more demanding application of the same discipline. His magic reinforces what his body can do; it doesn't replace his natural athleticism.

“Let the heart hold firm. Let the body answer.”

Limits: The enhancement is temporary. Sustaining it consumes magical energy, and pushing too far leaves him exhausted. Weakening another person's body requires concentration and is more difficult than reinforcing himself.
Ancestral perception · Specialized

5. Bloodline Sight

By focusing on a blood sample or a person's magical blood signature, Donta can perceive impressions of ancestry, kinship, inherited magical traits, and connections between bloodlines. Blood is a record of connection: a sample might reveal that two strangers are related, that a lineage carries a magical inheritance, or that a supposed family connection is inconsistent with what he senses.

“What was carried before, let it be known once more.”

Limits: He doesn't automatically receive a complete family tree, names, memories, or a person's entire history. Old, mixed, magically altered, or unfamiliar bloodlines may yield incomplete impressions.
Blood detection · Focused perception

6. The Red Awareness

When he deliberately concentrates, Donta can sense the presence of blood in his surroundings. He may notice a bleeding person behind a door, detect traces left at a scene, or recognize that blood is present where it shouldn't be. It's a quiet sensory ability rather than a dramatic vision.

No incantation required; a controlled breath and deliberate focus are sufficient.

Limits: It doesn't grant automatic omniscience or exact location tracking. Distance, barriers, environmental interference, and magical concealment can obscure the signal. He cannot identify every person in a crowd simply by sensing blood.

II. His Ritual and Binding Magic

Blood-binding oath · Ritual working

7. The Crimson Covenant

Donta establishes a magical oath between participants using blood as the connection. The oath can carry a consequence if a defined promise is broken, such as a warning, a loss of magical advantage, or a curse that activates under specified conditions.

“Let this promise outlive the moment. Let its breaking carry its own answer.”

Limits: A powerful oath needs a clear condition, preparation, and an anchor. It cannot simply manufacture genuine loyalty, guarantee that someone tells the truth about everything, or override every form of resistance. Breaking a strong oath may require outside magical intervention.
Ritual stabilization · Advanced

8. The Blood Anchor

Donta uses his own blood as a magical anchor to stabilize, extend, or preserve a working. The anchor gives a ritual a personal point of connection, helping it remain coherent after he has stopped actively directing every part of it. It can also help him recognize interference with a working he has established.

“What I have marked, remember me. What I have bound, remain.”

Limits: An anchor is not unlimited remote control. Distance, interference, time, and the complexity of the working all matter. Stronger anchors require greater preparation and personal cost.
Protection breaking · High-cost

9. Red Breach

Donta exploits the connection between blood, sacrifice, and magical protection to weaken a ward or barrier. Rather than simply overpowering every defense, he searches for a weakness and uses blood magic to force it open.

“Every wall has a seam. Every seal has a price.”

Limits: This is not a universal counterspell. Powerful protections may demand extensive preparation, an appropriate blood sacrifice, knowledge of the ward, and sustained effort. Some enchantments may resist him entirely.
Long-lasting curse · High-cost

10. The Inherited Malison

Donta lays a curse through a bloodline connection, allowing a prepared working to persist beyond the immediate encounter. In particularly demanding cases, the curse can be designed to follow a lineage across generations. This is his long-game magic: patient, deliberate, and meant to have consequences long after he has left the room.

“Blood carries what time cannot bury. Let the debt remember its name.”

Limits: Generational curses are among his most demanding workings. They require substantial preparation, a strong connection to the target, and an enduring magical anchor. They are not instantaneous, effortless, or impossible to remove.
Enchantment reinforcement · Specialized

11. The Red Consecration

By using a small amount of his own blood as a magical catalyst, Donta reinforces a compatible enchantment, ward, charm, or ritual. His blood acts as a personal signature, adding force and tying the work more closely to him.

“By what is mine, let this hold.”

Limits: It only strengthens workings that can accept his magical affinity. It doesn't make every charm indestructible or grant him the specialized abilities of another witch's discipline. Repeated use compounds his exhaustion.
Familiar-assisted working · Collaborative

12. Serpent’s Witness

Donta works with his familiar, Òṣùmàrè, to stabilize a ritual or detect disturbances in its surroundings. The python serves as a secondary magical anchor, while the familiar bond gives Donta impressions of its emotional state and basic intentions. When another supernatural presence enters the vicinity, Òṣùmàrè may alert him before he identifies the intruder himself.

“Òṣùmàrè, stay beside me. Let nothing unseen pass without notice.”

Limits: The python is a partner, not an all-knowing magical alarm. Its sensitivity gives warnings rather than perfect identification, and it can be distracted, overwhelmed, or kept away from a ritual.

III. What Makes Donta Dangerous

Information

Bloodline Sight · Red Awareness

These abilities help him notice connections, recognize clues, and understand when something is wrong.

Immediate control

Vein Command · The Unwounded Agony · The Red Stillness

They let him influence a confrontation without needing spectacular displays of magic.

Lasting consequences

The Crimson Covenant · Blood Anchor · Inherited Malison

These workings establish obligations and effects that persist beyond a single scene.

Preparation and defense

Red Breach · Red Consecration · Serpent’s Witness

They make him harder to surprise, ward against, or casually outmaneuver when he has time to prepare.

IV. Roleplay Balance

Small effects, lower cost

Small, immediate effects are generally easier than complex, sustained ones.

Control takes effort

Manipulating another person's blood is harder than reinforcing his own body or sensing nearby blood.

Preparation matters

Ritual space, ingredients, a blood connection, and a suitable anchor can improve the reliability of major workings.

Blood is not unlimited knowledge

He can discover impressions of ancestry without automatically learning every secret or memory.

One innate discipline

Donta can study general witchcraft, but he doesn't automatically gain a Life Witch's healing mastery, a Soothsayer's prophetic gifts, or an Arcane Witch's versatility.

Overuse has consequences

Fatigue, diminished concentration, and difficulty sustaining complex magic give other characters opportunities to resist or interrupt him.

Major workings deserve scenes

A generational curse or the destruction of a formidable protective barrier should be a significant working, not a casual gesture.
