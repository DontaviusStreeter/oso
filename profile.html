Horror RP 2: Personality

He's confident, street-smart, observant and naturally guarded. He doesn't walk around constantly talking about magic or trying to prove he's a witch. He's more likely to quietly watch a situation unfold and decide whether intervention is necessary.

Patient

He understands that blood magic often requires control rather than brute force.

Observant

Bloodlines, ancestry and magical connections make information particularly valuable to him.

Strategic

He prefers having leverage before making a move.

Protective

Once someone is considered part of his circle, he takes that connection seriously.

Intimidating

He doesn't need to threaten someone when his discipline itself can be intimidating.

Practical

He favors magic that produces a tangible result rather than elaborate magical displays.

Horror RP 3: Discipline

Blood Magic

Donta was born with an innate affinity for Blood Magic, a discipline that manifested naturally as his witchcraft began developing. While he can learn and practice general witchcraft like any other witch, blood magic is where his natural talent is strongest. His magic is not theatrical or overly elaborate. Donta tends toward practical, deliberate applications of his craft. He views blood as both a biological substance and a magical connection between people.

Meaning

Donta doesn't see Blood Magic as inherently evil. To him, blood represents connection, inheritance, life, debt, and consequence. That makes Blood Magic particularly attractive to someone like Donta, who naturally values information and leverage.

Blood tells him

Blood tells him where someone came from. Blood can bind two people together. Blood can strengthen a promise. Blood can expose what someone tries to hide.

Horror RP 4: Abilities

  • Manipulate another person's movements through their blood.
  • Cause intense pain without leaving visible physical injuries.
  • Stop or accelerate bleeding.
  • Strengthen or weaken the body.
  • Use blood as a component in rituals.
  • Break magical protections through blood sacrifice.
  • Detect bloodlines and ancestry.
  • Create blood-binding oaths.
  • Create long-lasting curses.
  • Use blood as a magical anchor during rituals.
  • Sense the presence of blood nearby when actively concentrating.
  • Strengthen certain enchantments through the use of his own blood.

Horror RP 5: Strengths & Weaknesses

Strengths

  • Strong natural Blood Magic affinity
  • physically imposing
  • intelligent and observant
  • good at practical spellcraft
  • skilled with rituals & cursework
  • capable of learning broad general witchcraft
  • strong familiar bond potential

Weaknesses

  • Overuse can exhaust him. Powerful blood magic requires preparation, ingredients, physical blood, ritual space or sustained concentration depending on what he is attempting. Manipulating another person's blood is considerably more demanding than performing a simple charm. Breaking powerful magical protections may require substantial preparation and blood sacrifice. Despite being capable of learning general witchcraft, he will never naturally possess the same innate advantage as witches whose primary disciplines correspond to those particular forms of magic

Horror RP 6: Witchcraft

Overview

While Blood Witch is his natural discipline, Donta has spent considerable time learning general witchcraft.

Herbalism

Used for potions, brews, salves, powders, incense and ritual mixtures.

Enchantments

Protective charms, personal wards, enchanted objects and practical magical tools.

Curses & Hexes

He specializes in incorporating blood into otherwise conventional cursework.

Ritual Magic

Particularly rituals involving candles, herbs, crystals, written symbols and offerings.

Protective Magic

Wards designed to protect people, rooms, objects and the Sanctum.

Potioncraft

Especially brews designed around physical enhancement, protection, attraction, pain relief and magical effects.

Specialization

He is not naturally gifted in every branch of witchcraft. Blood remains where his strongest supernatural ability lies.

Donta's familiar is a python, chosen not simply because snakes are traditionally associated with witchcraft, but because the animal complements his particular magical affinity.
